FAQ

Is this allowed?

Yes. The channel is yours, the videos are real uploads, and YouTube's own rules decide monetization. Your channel has to meet YouTube's requirements (subscriber count, watch hours, and policy compliance) before it earns. We do the publishing work. We do not fake views or engagement, because that gets channels removed.

Do you need my password?

The connect form asks for your Google account email and password because channel setup signs in on your behalf to create and configure the channel. You can revoke that access at any time by changing your password or removing app access in your Google account settings, and we stop immediately.

What do credits pay for?

Video generation and uploads. Each pack lists its credit amount. When your balance runs low, scheduled releases pause until you top up.

Are the earnings on the dashboard exact?

No. They are estimates based on views and typical revenue per thousand views. YouTube reports final numbers in AdSense on its own schedule, usually monthly.
